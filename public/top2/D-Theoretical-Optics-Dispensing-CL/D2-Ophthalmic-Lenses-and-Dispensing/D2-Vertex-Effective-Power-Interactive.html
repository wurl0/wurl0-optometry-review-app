1. Slide the lens

The eye is fixed on the right. Move the lens toward or away from the cornea. Its labeled power never changes, but the power that actually reaches the cornea does. Watch the effective bar close on the labeled bar as the lens nears the eye.

At the cornea (0 mm)toward the eye ←Far (20 mm)
Toward the eye, minus gains, plus drains. Away, the other way. Effective power always slides toward the labeled power as the lens nears the cornea.

2. Why it works

  • A plus lens focuses light behind itself. Held far away, that converging bundle is tighter by the time it reaches the cornea, so it acts stronger than the label. Slide it in and the effect drains back toward the label.
  • A minus lens diverges light. Far away, some of that spread is lost before the cornea, so it acts weaker. Slide it in and the effect gains back toward the label.
  • Formula: F_eff = F / (1 − d·F), with d in metres. At d = 0 the factor is 1, so effective power equals the label.
  • Spec → contact lens moves the lens to the cornea. The equivalent CL power is this effective power: more plus for a plus Rx, less minus for a minus Rx.
