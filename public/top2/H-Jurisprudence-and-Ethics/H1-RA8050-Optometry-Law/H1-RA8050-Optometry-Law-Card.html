Card01RA 8050

One law runs the profession: RA 8050

The Revised Optometry Law defines what you may do, who regulates you, how you get and keep a license, and what will get it taken away. Learn the scope, the Board, the licensure path, and the prohibited acts, and the jurisprudence questions become quotations.

01 Scope of practice

RA 8050 defines the practice of optometry broadly: examining the eye and visual system, refraction, prescribing and dispensing lenses, vision therapy, low-vision care, using diagnostic pharmaceutical agents, and establishing an optometric clinic all count.

Two chair-side rules

Every prescription must show your license (registration) number, that is the disclosure of authority to practice. And using the title "Registered Optometrist" without a PRC license is the prohibited act of unauthorized assumption of a professional title.

02 The Board of Optometry

Who regulates you

A three-member Board (a chairman and two members), appointed by the President from a list, serving a 3-year term. Members must be Filipino registered optometrists of good moral character, and must not be officers of any optometric organization (that is a disqualification).

Its powers

Administer the law, prepare and update the licensure examinations, issue and revoke certificates, and investigate violations. A majority (two members) is a quorum and the minimum vote to decide a case.

03 Becoming and staying licensed

The rules that trip people up

Three consecutive failures require a refresher course in an accredited institution before retaking. The oath comes before the Certificate of Registration is issued. A professional who is 65 or older at renewal is exempt from CPD.

04 Diagnostic pharmaceutical agents

Authority and agents

RA 8050 authorizes optometrists to use diagnostic pharmaceutical agents such as tropicamide. An optometrist admitted before RA 8050 who wants DPA authority must complete a pharmacology course and pass a certification examination. Using an unlisted or unauthorized drug is a prohibited act.

05 Prohibited acts, penalties, and integration

Prohibited actExample
Illegal practicePracticing without a license, or endorsing an unregistered product
Unauthorized titleCalling yourself "Registered Optometrist" without a license
Unauthorized DPA useA drug outside the authorized list / without certification
Improper advertising / solicitation"Cheapest eyeglasses," "No. 1 eye doctor," canvassers and promoters
Consequences and membership

Prohibited acts are punishable by fine, imprisonment, or both. Conviction of a crime involving moral turpitude is grounds to suspend or revoke, after due process. RA 8050 also encourages professional integration, membership in the accredited national organization, and a foreign optometrist may sit the exam only by reciprocity (or practice under a special permit from the Board and PRC).

08 Spaced-repetition deck

Into the review queue, 1 / 2 / 3 / 7 / 14 day ladder. Answer out loud before you flip.

  1. What is RA 8050? The Revised Optometry Law, defining practice, the Board, licensure, and penalties.
  2. Does refraction + prescribing + vision therapy count as practice? Yes, all are acts constituting the practice of optometry.
  3. Board of Optometry composition and term? Three members (a chairman and two), a 3-year term.
  4. Who is disqualified from the Board? An officer of an optometric organization.
  5. Passing the licensure exam? General average at least 75% AND no grade below 50%.
  6. After three consecutive failures? Complete a refresher course in an accredited institution before retaking.
  7. Oath vs certificate order? Take the oath first; the Certificate of Registration is issued only after.
  8. PIC renewal cycle and CPD? Every 3 years with CPD units; those 65 and older are exempt from CPD.
  9. A DPA authorized by RA 8050? Tropicamide (a diagnostic agent).
  10. Pre-RA 8050 optometrist wanting DPA authority? Complete a pharmacology course and pass a certification exam.
  11. Penalty for prohibited acts? Fine, imprisonment, or both.
  12. Foreign optometrist eligibility? By reciprocity, or a special permit approved by the Board and PRC.
