Card03Practice mgmt

Register in order, then pay the right tax

Opening a clinic is a checklist: register the business through the right agencies in order, then handle a short list of taxes and the FDA license to sell devices. Learn the sequence, the tax formulas, and the FDA rules, and the practice-management items are arithmetic.

01 The registration sequence

For a sole proprietorship, register in this order, name first, then local permits, then tax, then the device license.

The registrars

DTI = business name (sole proprietor); SEC = corporations. BIR gives the TIN and requires registered books of accounts and official receipts before you operate. The barangay clearance needs a sanitary permit for health compliance. Expanding to another city means a new business permit there.

02 The taxes

TaxRule
Professional tax (PTR)Annual, paid to the LGU; a professional employed only by the government is exempt
Community tax (cedula)Individual: basic P5 + P1 per P1,000 income; corporate basic P500. Due by February; late = interest
VAT vs percentage taxSales below P3M = non-VAT, 3% percentage tax; above P3M = VAT 12%
Rental (EWT)5% expanded withholding tax on rent
Income tax returnFiled by residents and locals; an OFW's foreign-source income is exempt from filing
Worked computations

Community tax on P75,000/month (P900,000/yr): 5 + 900 = P905. Rental EWT on P16,789: 16,789 x 0.05 = P839.45. P2.5M sales: below 3M, so non-VAT at 3%. Corporate cedula basic = P500.

03 Selling devices and contact lenses: FDA

The FDA requirements

To retail contact lenses or optical devices, the outlet needs an FDA License to Operate (LTO) as a medical-device retailer, must designate a registered optometrist as the Qualified Person, and may dispense contact lenses only on a valid ophthalmic prescription (contact lenses are medical devices). The initial LTO is valid 2 years.

Products and inspections

Products need market authorization: a CMDN (Certificate of Medical Device Notification) and CPR (Certificate of Product Registration). FDA may conduct routine or spot-check inspections during business hours, no appointment required.

04 Setting up the clinic

A few operational facts

Design the layout around examination rooms and patient flow. Stock the frame inventory to the demographics of your patient population, not the rep's pitch. Board Resolution No. 11 sets a minimum examination-room area and the required instruments (keratometer for contact-lens work, tonometer and fields for DPA practice). Semi-rimless nylon frames need a lens groover at the bench.

07 Spaced-repetition deck

Into the review queue, 1 / 2 / 3 / 7 / 14 day ladder. Answer out loud before you flip.

  1. First registration step for a sole proprietorship? Register the business name with the DTI (SEC for corporations).
  2. What must BIR give you before operating? A TIN, plus registered books of accounts and official receipts.
  3. Expanding to another city? Obtain a new business (mayor's) permit from that LGU.
  4. Professional tax (PTR)? An annual professional tax paid to the LGU; government-only employees are exempt.
  5. Individual community tax? Basic P5 + P1 per P1,000 of income (corporate basic P500); due by February.
  6. Community tax on P900,000 income? P905 (5 + 900).
  7. VAT threshold and the non-VAT rate? P3M; below it, non-VAT at 3% percentage tax; above, VAT 12%.
  8. Rental withholding tax? 5% EWT (P16,789 gives P839.45).
  9. FDA requirements to sell contact lenses? An LTO as a device retailer, a registered-optometrist Qualified Person, and a valid ophthalmic prescription.
  10. Initial FDA LTO validity? 2 years.
  11. Product market-authorization documents? CMDN and CPR.
  12. Can FDA inspect without an appointment? Yes, routine or spot checks during business hours.
