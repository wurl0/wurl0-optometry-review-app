Card05Tax & practice

Practice management: the taxes and the rules a new OD must know

Every ethics miss on the preboard was practice management: the Philippine taxes that attach to an optical clinic, plus a few board-exam and professional rules. These are exact figures and thresholds, so this card leads with the numbers. Rates can change, so treat the values as of current PRC and BIR guidance.

01 The taxes on an optical practice

ItemWhat it is / the figure
Professional Tax Receipt (PTR)annual regulatory fee paid to the local government (LGU)
Community Tax Certificate (cedula), individualbasic P5 plus P1 for every P1,000 of income
Community tax, corporation (added basic)P500 basic
Clinic with P2.5M annual salesnon-VAT registered; 3% percentage tax
Expanded withholding tax on rent5% of the rental
Late community-tax payment (after February)subject to interest
The two computations the board loves

Cedula on P75,000/month: annual income P900,000, so P1 per P1,000 = P900, plus the P5 basic = P905. EWT on P16,789 rent: 5% = P839.45. The VAT threshold is P3M, so P2.5M sales stays non-VAT and pays the 3% percentage tax.

Registration and expansion

Register your books of accounts with the BIR to record transactions. To open in another city or municipality, get a new business permit from that LGU, not just an amendment of the old one.

02 Board-exam and licensure rules

SituationResult

A high average does not save a low subject

Passing needs both a passing general average and no single rating below the floor. A 48% in one subject fails the candidate even with a 76% average. And a failed practical is not conditioned; the whole examination is retaken.

03 Professional conduct and patient rights

ScenarioCorrect action / classification
prescribing or endorsing a product not registered with FDA/DTIunlawful practice
a paying patient asks for a copy of the spectacle prescriptionrelease it; it is part of the patient's records
The prescription belongs to the patient

A patient who has paid for the examination is entitled to a copy of the prescription. Withholding it to force a purchase is an ethics violation, and endorsing an unregistered product is unlawful practice.

05 Spaced-repetition deck

  1. What is the Professional Tax Receipt? An annual regulatory fee paid to the LGU.
  2. Individual cedula formula? P5 basic plus P1 per P1,000 of income.
  3. Cedula on P75,000/month income? P905 (P900 + P5).
  4. EWT rate on rent? 5% (P839.45 on P16,789).
  5. P2.5M sales: VAT status and tax? Non-VAT; 3% percentage tax (VAT threshold is P3M).
  6. Expanding to another city needs? A new business permit from that LGU.
  7. Registered with BIR to record transactions? Books of accounts.
  8. 76% GWA but 48% in one subject? Failed (no rating below the floor).
  9. Units needed to renew the PIC? 15 CPD/CPE units.
  10. Endorsing an unregistered product? Unlawful practice.
  11. Patient asks for the prescription? Release it; it is part of the records.
