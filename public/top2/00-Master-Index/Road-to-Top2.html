Four rules that fit how you learn

Retrieval over rereading

Rebuild from a blank page. Rereading feels productive and isn't. The struggle to pull it back from memory is the part that sticks, especially given where your retrieval muscle is right now.

The error log is the plan

Wrong answers are the syllabus. A miss you understood beats ten questions you already knew. Feed every miss back into the deck.

Formulas get the why

Never formula plus sample problem. Meaning, origin, analogy, why this form, cross-links. Pharma and Visual Bio reward the mechanism, not the memorized line.

Plain words first

Explain it before you name it. Say the mechanism in ordinary language, then attach the clinical term. You know the machinery; the labels come after.
