CBLE Interface Cheat Sheet

How the PRC Computer-Based Licensure Exam screen works — so nothing on exam day is a surprise. Personal study notes.

Screen 1 — The exam screen

Header info box (left)

Your name, the exam name, and the subject. Just confirms you are in the right test.

TIMETime Remaining (right)

Live countdown in red. Below it: Questions Answered and Total Number of TIQ (total items). Your pacing dashboard — read "answered vs total" against the clock.

Question / Choices

One item at a time. Four options (A–D), click the radio to answer. Selecting an option bumps the Answered count.

NAVPrevious · Next · First · Last

Step one item at a time, or jump straight to the start or end of the set.

▦ MAPQuestion Roadmap (orange)

The big one. Opens an overview to jump to any question directly and see what is answered or bookmarked. Use this instead of clicking Next 100 times.

☆ MARKBookmark this item

The "flag for review" button. Bookmark anything you are unsure of, then return to just those via the Roadmap.

👁 Hide Screen

Blurs/hides the screen for privacy or a quick pause without exposing your answers. Does not stop the clock.

✔ ENDSubmit (green)

Ends and locks in the whole exam. You hit this once, at the very end. Do not touch it until the Roadmap shows no red left.

Screen 2 — The Question Roadmap

● GREEN (with ●) = already answered
RED = not yet answered
◉ Blue bookmark = an item you marked for review
Click any question number to jump straight to it. Close returns you to the current item — it does not submit.

The two-pass strategy this interface rewards

  1. First pass: answer everything you know fast (turns green). Bookmark the hard ones (blue icon) and keep moving — never stall on one item.
  2. Second pass: open the Roadmap and hit only your bookmarked and remaining red items, easiest first.
  3. Pace check: watch Questions Answered / Total TIQ against Time Remaining. "8 reds left, 3 minutes" tells you to speed up.
  4. Before Submit: reopen the Roadmap and confirm zero red. No penalty for guessing — a red is a guaranteed zero, a guess is not.
The one habit that saves points: the exam is not done when you reach the last question — it is done when the Roadmap has no red left. That is the trap that costs marks.
